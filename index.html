Built for North East India's Terrain

North East India is one of the most landslide-prone regions in the world. Steep slopes, heavy monsoon rainfall, seismic activity, and fragile geology combine to create conditions where communities need early warning — not after the fact.

Raksha360 uses real rainfall data from the Open-Meteo Archive API (2025–2026), gradient boosting machine learning, and explainable AI to give both citizens and authorities a clear, actionable picture of risk.

Risk scores are model estimates. Not certified forecasts — always follow official government advisories.
